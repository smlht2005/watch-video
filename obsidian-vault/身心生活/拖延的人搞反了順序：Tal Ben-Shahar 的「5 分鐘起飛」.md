### 主題 1：動力不在行動之前，而在行動之後（00:00–00:33）

![00:00](../_attachments/DeUFrNTDHFs/frame_0004.jpg)

**重點：** 拖延的人以為要先有動力才能行動；不拖延的人相反，先行動，動力隨後而來。

**標籤：** #拖延 #動力 #行動

In simple terms, procrastinators believe that motivation must come before action. They feel they need to be inspired to want to act before they begin. Those who procrastinate less tend to see it the other way around. They understand that action comes first and motivation follows. In other words, you begin and the inspiration often catches up.

**中譯：** 簡單說，愛拖延的人以為必須先有動力，才能行動。他們覺得得先受到激勵、想做了，才會開始。比較不拖延的人，看法正好相反：他們明白是先行動，動力隨後才來。換句話說，你先開始，靈感常常就追上來了。

### 主題 2：5 分鐘起飛策略（00:33–01:12）

![00:33](../_attachments/DeUFrNTDHFs/frame_0008.jpg)

**重點：** 研究者常建議的解藥：先做一小段（哪怕只有幾分鐘）就產生動能；講者自己不想寫作的日子也這麼做。

**標籤：** #5 分鐘起飛 #動能

As an antidote to procrastination, researchers often recommend the five-minute take-off strategy. The idea is simple. Start doing the thing you've been putting off, even if only for a few minutes. This creates a momentum. Five minutes often lead to another five, and then another, setting off an upward spiral of action. I use this technique often myself. On days when I don't feel like writing, I rely on the five-minute takeoff. More often than not, once I begin, I find my rhythm and can continue for much longer.

**中譯：** 作為拖延的解藥，研究者常建議「5 分鐘起飛」策略。做法很簡單：開始做你一直拖著不做的事，哪怕只做幾分鐘。這會產生動能。五分鐘常常會變成下一個五分鐘，再下一個，形成向上的行動螺旋。我自己也常用這招。不想寫作的日子，我就靠「5 分鐘起飛」。多半時候，一旦開始，我就找到節奏，能做得久很多。

### 主題 3：5 秒法則與漸進式改變（01:12–01:50）

![01:12](../_attachments/DeUFrNTDHFs/frame_0012.jpg)

**重點：** Mel Robbins 的 5 秒法則：從五倒數到一就行動；引用 Jim Loehr 與 Tony Schwartz：漸進式改變勝過野心勃勃的失敗。

**標籤：** #5 秒法則 #Mel Robbins #漸進式改變

Mel Robbins takes this idea a step further with what she calls the five-second rule. When there is something you want to do, whether it's getting out of bed, starting a workout, or making a call, you count down from five to one, and then you act. As Jim Loehr and Tony Schwartz put it, incremental change is better than ambitious failure. Success feeds on itself.

**中譯：** Mel Robbins 把這個想法再推進一步，稱為「5 秒法則」。當你有件想做的事——不論是起床、開始運動，或是打一通電話——你從五倒數到一，然後就行動。就像 Jim Loehr 和 Tony Schwartz 說的：漸進式的改變，勝過野心勃勃的失敗。成功會自我滋養。
